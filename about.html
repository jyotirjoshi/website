We Built EXIM ADVICE Because Indian Businesses Deserve a Real Export Partner

There's no shortage of exporters in India. What's often missing is the right guidance — practical, honest, and tailored to your specific business.

EXIM ADVICE was built to fill that gap. We're a professional export-import consultancy dedicated to helping Indian entrepreneurs, manufacturers, traders, and SMEs build sustainable international trade operations.

We've worked with businesses starting from absolute zero — no IEC, no buyers, no documentation experience — and helped them reach international markets professionally. And we've worked with established exporters who needed to break into new countries or fix gaps in their existing processes.

Our approach is always the same: understand your business first, then build a plan that actually works for you.
