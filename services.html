2. Export Business Setup

Starting an export business involves several registrations and digital infrastructure pieces. We handle everything so you can focus on your product production.

From getting your Import Export Code (IEC) to launching a website designed for international importers — our team executes all requirements seamlessly.

  • IEC (Importer Exporter Code) Registration
  • MSME/Udyam Registration
  • AD Code registration with bank
  • ICEGATE portal setup and AD registration
  • APEDA / FSSAI / RCMC registrations
  • Professional logo and brand design
  • Export-focused website and email setup
  • Trade portal registration & digital brochures

4. Logistics & Shipping Coordination

Shipping your first container can feel overwhelming. We simplify the logistics process, coordinating with freight forwarders and Custom House Agents (CHAs) on your behalf.

  • Freight forwarder selection and auditing
  • Shipping line booking & space allocation
  • Container type assessment (FCL vs LCL cargo)
  • Custom House Agent (CHA) coordination
  • Pre-shipment port customs documentation
  • Marine cargo insurance selection guidance
  • Export port custom clearance processing checks
